All our software is open source and available for free.

Source code can be found in the git repositories and binary packages are available in "Releases" section and/or in 
respective app store for each platform.

Simplest way to find us is to open the app store and search for **"tigase"**.

To find us on [GitHub](https://github.com/tigase), and browse our projects. Go to "Releases" section to find binary 
packages.
