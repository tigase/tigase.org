All our software is open source and available for free.

Community around our software and projects is essential part of our activity. We make our software open source and 
publicly available to be used by people. We make it better based on community feedback, suggestions and bug reports.

You can join our community and reach out to our team any time.

Go to [GitHub](https://github.com/tigase), and browse our projects. To contact us open a new issue within the 
project of your interest. Our team responds as soon as possible.
