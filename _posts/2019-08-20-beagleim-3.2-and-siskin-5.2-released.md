New versions of XMPP clients for Apple's mobile and desktop platforms have been released.

They include following changes:

- UI improvements

  - visual tweaks in the main application view
  - possibility to select message grouping logic and it's colouring
  - better exposure of contact details from it's vCard

- support for XMPP URI links - so, for example, you will be join us in [xmpp:tigase@muc.tigase.org?join](xmpp:tigase@muc.tigase.org?join)
- improvements in audio and video calls

In addition, Siskin IM also improves significantly in push area:

- better compatibility with reliability
- notifications for groupchat rooms (on supported servers)

You can download both application from their respective app-stores: [Beagle IM](https://beagle.im/) from [macOS appstore](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/beagleim-by-tigase-inc/id1445349494) and [Siskin IM](https://siskin.im/) from [iOS appstore](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/tigase-messenger/id1153516838) and star them on GitHub: [Siskin IM on GitHub](https://github.com/tigase/siskin-im) and [Beagle IM on GitHub](https://github.com/tigase/beagle-im)
