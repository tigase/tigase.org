Tigase v7.1.5 has been released! This is a maintenance release for Tigase v7.1.4 with a few fixes and
updates. For changenotes for v7.1.0, visit [this link]({% post_url 2017-01-30-tigase-server-710-release %}) for detailed
changenotes.

Greetings users of Tigase!

Recently we underwent an upgrade on our servers, including the ones that hosted our public servers. However, during this upgrade there was an error in the vhost database wherein many registered vhosts became unavailable for a period of time.
We have since restored the previously registered vhosts, and all services should have returned to normal.
We apologize for any inconvenience, or interruptions you may have experienced during the upgrade and migration.
If your system has been adversely affected, or has not returned to normal use, please contact us at support@tigase.net.

We have been working hard to provide new features and stability to the Tigase XMPP software and related components. We hope that the features in upcoming versions of Tigase XMPP Server will be worth the wait.
We would also like to take this opportunity to mention another public project available:
Our Android messenger and Apple IOS client applications which can be found in the 
[Google Play store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.tigase.messenger.phone.pro&hl=en) or 
[Aple AppStore](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/tigase-messenger/id1153516838?mt=8). They have both been updated to 
provide new features like push notifications and automatic status management!

## Thank you for using Tigase!

Daniel Wisnewski
CSR, Tigase.net
