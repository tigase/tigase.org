For a long time we were using Redmine for all our project organising needs. However, our growing requirements made us look for alternatives.

Without further ado we present you [our support system - help.tigase.net](http://help.tigase.net/), central place were you can submit new ticket or ask community in public forums.

All our source code has been migrated do [GitHub - Tigase, Inc.](https://github.com/tigase/) and internally we are using YouTrack.
